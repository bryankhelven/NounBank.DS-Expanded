# Provenance and limitations

Scientific data authority is frozen by ORCH_RECON_000158. Productization is governed by ORCH_RECON_000159.

The target corpus is DANTEStocks only. Porttinari is not row-level authority for this resource.

The 564 new lexical entries are partitioned into 295 DUPB-backed and 269 LeGOS-positive without DUPB-backed authority. Twenty-three S occurrences retain typed non-exact WSD status. Ninety-four semantic exceptions are preserved outside the regular argument pipeline rather than being forced into an ordinary English-NomBank-aligned representation.

English NomBank mappings are alignment authority, not identity of the Portuguese semantic unit.
