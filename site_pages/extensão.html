Roleset id: extensão.01, Mapeamento para o inglês: extension.01

Roles:

  • Arg 0: agent, entity making something be longer
  • Arg 1: patient, thing getting longer
  • Arg 2: ext or tmp, by how much or until when
  • Arg 3: start point
  • Arg 4: end point
