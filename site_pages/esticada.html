Procedência do roleset

Papéis semânticos

  • Arg 0: AGENT, ENTITY MAKING SOMETHING BE LONGER
  • Arg 1: QUANTIFIED/THEME/PATIENT, THING GETTING LONGER
  • Arg 2: EXT OR TMP, BY HOW MUCH OR UNTIL WHEN
  • Arg 3: START POINT
  • Arg 4: END POINT
